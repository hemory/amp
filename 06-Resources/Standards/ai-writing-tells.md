# Anti-AI Writing Quality Gate

Use this standard for drafts the user will send, post, say, or publish. The goal is writing that sounds grounded in the actual situation, not polished generic filler.

## Rewrite if the draft has these tells

- Generic insight that could apply to any team or topic.
- Inflated abstraction without a concrete decision, artifact, person, or constraint.
- Over-balanced structure that avoids taking a position.
- Filler transitions such as "in today's fast-paced environment" or "it is important to note."
- Corporate-safe verbs with no lived context, such as "leverage," "drive alignment," or "unlock value" when a clearer verb exists.
- Fake nuance that says both sides matter without naming the real trade-off.
- Unsourced claims, metrics, quotes, dates, or named examples.
- A tone that sounds like a consultant summary rather than the user's channel and relationship.

## Quality bar

A usable draft should:

- Start from the real person, project, meeting, artifact, constraint, or decision.
- Carry a clear judgment or ask.
- Use the user's channel-specific register.
- Include only facts that are sourced or clearly labeled as assumptions.
- Sound like it was written for this exact situation.

If the draft could work for another topic by swapping nouns, rewrite it.
